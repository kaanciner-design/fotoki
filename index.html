Fotoki: Passport & ID Photo

Create passport, visa and ID photos in official sizes, right on your iPhone. All processing happens on your device.

Support

Questions, a missing document size, or a problem with your photo? Email kaanciner@icloud.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked

Will my photo be accepted? Fotoki applies the official size, head height and background rules and checks pose, lighting and eyes. Final acceptance is always decided by the issuing authority.

How do I print? Use "Print sheet 4×6 in" and print it as a standard 4×6 / 10×15 cm photo at any pharmacy or photo kiosk, then cut along the lines.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Fotoki.
