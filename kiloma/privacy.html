Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026 · App: Kiloma: GLP-1 Shot Tracker · Developer: Ciner Labs

Summary

Kiloma does not collect personal data. Your health diary stays on your devices. We run no servers that receive it and we cannot see it.

What you enter

Your treatment profile (medication, dose, schedule, start date, weights, height, goals, optional first name, prices), dose logs and injection sites, weigh-ins and measurements, daily check-ins (food noise, hunger, energy, mood, side effects, notes), food and water entries and supplies are stored only on your iPhone. You can export everything as CSV or delete it in More > Reset all data. Deleting the app also deletes it.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. It is protected by your Apple Account and is not accessible to us.

Food lookup

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, only the barcode number or search text is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free public food database, to return nutrition values. No identifiers or health data are sent. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Coach and food estimates

On supported devices the coach and the "Describe" food estimate use Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). Your messages and your data summary are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us or to third parties. Conversations are not saved after you close the coach.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, Kiloma writes the weight and waist you log to Apple Health and reads weight, waist, steps and workouts to chart progress and your strength-training goal. Health data is never used for advertising or data mining, never shared with third parties and never leaves your device through Kiloma.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes, live on the device. No images are saved or uploaded.

Face ID

The optional app lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication. Kiloma never receives your biometric data.

Notifications

Dose, check-in, weigh-in and refill reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Subscription status is checked with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Kiloma contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Kiloma is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data for us to access, correct or delete; you control all of it on your device and in your own iCloud. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and delete it once resolved.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
