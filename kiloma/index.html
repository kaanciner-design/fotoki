Kiloma: GLP-1 Shot Tracker

Kiloma is a private diary for people on GLP-1 medication (such as Mounjaro®, Wegovy®, Ozempic® and Zepbound®). Log doses with injection-site rotation, see your estimated medication level, track weight, protein, food noise and side effects, and bring a clear report to your prescriber. Kiloma is independent and not affiliated with any manufacturer.

FAQ

How is the medication level calculated? Kiloma applies a standard pharmacokinetic model (first-order absorption and elimination) with published half-life and absorption times to the doses you log. It is an estimate to help you see patterns, not a blood test.

Does Kiloma tell me what dose to take? No. Kiloma only records the dose your prescriber chose. It never suggests starting, changing or skipping a dose.

How do I cancel my subscription? In Kiloma open More > Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone? Open Kiloma > More > Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is my data private? Yes. There is no account. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Kiloma medical advice? No. Kiloma is a personal diary, not a medical device. Always follow your prescriber's advice.
