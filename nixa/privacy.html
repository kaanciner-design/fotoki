Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026 · App: Nixa: Quit Zyn & Nicotine · Developer: Ciner Labs

Summary

Nixa does not collect, store on our servers or share any personal data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

What you enter

Your habit profile (product type, amounts, prices, plan settings, optional first name, reasons for quitting), each logged use, cravings and notes are stored only on your device, in an app container shared with Nixa's own widgets. We do not run servers that receive this data and we cannot see it. You can export it as CSV or delete all of it in Settings at any time. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Craving coach

On supported devices the coach uses Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). Your messages are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us or to third parties. On other devices the coach uses built-in, rule-based replies. Conversations are not saved after you close the coach.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, Nixa writes your breathing exercises to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes and may read Mindful Minutes. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with third parties and never leaves your device through Nixa.

Face ID

The optional app lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication. Nixa never receives your biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Nixa contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Nixa is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights (GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data for us to access, correct or delete; you control all of it on your device. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply and delete it once resolved.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
