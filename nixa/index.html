FAQ

How does the step-down plan work? Your daily limit drops a little every week until it reaches zero. The gap timer spreads your allowance across the day so you naturally wait longer between uses.

How do I cancel my subscription? In Nixa open Settings > Manage subscription, or open iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone? Open Nixa > Settings > Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is my data private? Yes. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Nixa medical advice? No. Nixa is a habit tracking and motivation tool, not a medical device. For nicotine replacement therapy or health concerns, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.
