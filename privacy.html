Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026 · App: Fotoki: Passport & ID Photo · Developer: Ciner Labs

Summary

Fotoki does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your photos never leave your device.

Photos and camera

Photos you take or select are processed entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision and Core Image) to detect your face, measure head size and replace the background. Photos are not uploaded to any server, and we have no access to them. Finished photos are saved only where you choose (Photos, Files, or a share destination).

Face data

Face detection is used only to position and check your ID photo during the editing session. No face data, biometric template or measurement is stored after you leave the editor, and none is transmitted off your device or used for identification, tracking or advertising.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Fotoki contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Fotoki is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights (GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data for us to access, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply and delete it once resolved.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
