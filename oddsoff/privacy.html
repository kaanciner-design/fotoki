Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026 · App: OddsOff: Quit Gambling & Bets · Developer: Ciner Labs

Summary

OddsOff does not collect personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. We run no servers that receive it and we cannot see it.

What you enter

Your profile (optional first name, what you gambled on, frequency, typical stake and monthly loss estimate, reasons, message to yourself, payday), your last bet date, urges, slips, evening check-ins, debts and your signed commitment are stored only on your iPhone, in a container shared with OddsOff's own widgets and extensions. You can export everything as CSV or delete it in More > Erase all data. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Screen Time (Family Controls)

If you allow it, OddsOff uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) with individual authorization on your own device to hide gambling apps, block gambling websites and show a shield screen. Apps and websites you pick are represented by opaque tokens that OddsOff cannot read. Screen Time data is processed by iOS on your device and is never sent to us or to third parties. OddsOff counts how many times you tapped a shield's buttons, stored on your device only.

Safari content blocker

The Safari extensions contain a fixed list of gambling domains. Safari applies it on your device; OddsOff never sees your browsing.

Coach

On supported devices the coach uses Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). Your messages and a short summary of your own data are processed on your iPhone and not sent to us or to third parties. Conversations are not saved.

Face ID

The optional app lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication. OddsOff never receives biometric data.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders and the Game Night Guard are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive payment details. Subscription status is checked with StoreKit on your device.

Analytics, ads and tracking

OddsOff contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

OddsOff is intended for adults.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA, LGPD)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data for us to access, correct or delete; you control all of it on your device. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and delete it once resolved.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
