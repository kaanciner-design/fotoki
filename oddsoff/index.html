FAQ

How does the blocker work? With your permission, OddsOff uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls, individual authorization) on your own iPhone to hide known gambling apps, block gambling sites in all browsers and show your personal shield on apps you choose. Your app usage never leaves your device.

How do I turn on the Safari shield? Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions, then turn on OddsOff Web Shield 1 and 2.

What is the commitment lock? An optional setting you choose. While it is on, turning blocks off requires a cooldown you picked (24 hours, 3 days or 7 days) and OddsOff can't be deleted. You can always remove OddsOff's Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

How do I cancel my subscription? OddsOff > More > Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is my data private? Yes. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. See the Privacy Policy.

Is OddsOff a treatment? No. It is a self-help tool. If gambling is harming you, please contact a helpline or a doctor.
